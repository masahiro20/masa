# AI trends predicted to take off late 2026-2027 (overseas voices, state as of Oct 2026)

Labels: [P] = primary source fetched/seen directly (firm or analyst's own page); [S] = secondary (press/blog summarizing); [U] = unverified/conflicting. Most search was snippet-level; items marked [S] should be checked against the original before being quoted.

## What are YC, a16z, Sequoia, Menlo etc. saying about the next wave? (candidate trends)

### Takeaway
VC voices converge on: AI-native service companies that sell finished work ("services-as-software"), agents as users of software/infrastructure, multi-agent orchestration inside enterprises, voice agents moving from wedge to full workflow, and agentic commerce rails. Solo/small-team tools for "100x solo founders" are explicitly requested by a16z Speedrun.

### Cited Findings
**T1. AI-native service companies / services-as-software**
- YC Summer 2026 RFS item 1.1: startups that sell completed work rather than software, replacing outsourced services in insurance brokerage, accounting, tax, audit, compliance, healthcare admin. [S: VC Cafe summary of YC RFS] — [VC Cafe](https://www.vccafe.com/requests-for-startups-summer-2026-edition/). Note: ycombinator.com/rfs fetch returned only the Fall 2026 edition. [P] for Fall item "AI-Native Compliance Infrastructure" (financial compliance, fewer specialized headcount) — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Sequoia (partner Julien Bek) reportedly frames "copilots" (sell tools) vs "autopilots" (sell outcomes); enterprises spend ~$6 on services per $1 on software. [S/U: third-party summary; original essay not found in my searches] — [Linas's Newsletter](https://linas.substack.com/p/sequoiathesis), [Dealroom summary](https://alternative-protein.dealroom.co/news/feed/sequoia-ai-will-replace-software-tools-by-executing-work-not-assisting-humans)
- Timing: already funded now; YC asks for it in 2026 batches -> mainstream 2027. Entry for small team: moderate (vertical, needs domain licences/liability in tax, insurance, audit).

**T2. Software/infrastructure for agents as users ("agent-native")**
- YC Summer 2026 item 1.3 "Software for Agents": APIs, MCPs, CLIs, machine-readable docs, identity, permissions, payments; quote "The next trillion users on the internet won't be people, they'll be AI agents." [S] — [VC Cafe](https://www.vccafe.com/requests-for-startups-summer-2026-edition/)
- YC Fall 2026: "Self-Maintaining APIs", "Proving You're Human" (human verification trust layer), "A Cloud for Small Software" (cloud for small agent-built apps), "Multiplayer AI". [P] — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- a16z Big Ideas 2026 Part 1: agent traffic looks like a DDoS to legacy databases/rate-limiters; need agent-speed infrastructure. [S: search summary of a16z page] — [a16z Part 1](https://a16z.com/newsletter/big-ideas-2026-part-1/)

**T3. Enterprise multi-agent orchestration / "systems of coordination" / Company Brain**
- a16z Big Ideas 2026 Part 2: enterprises shift from isolated tools to multi-agent systems needing "systems of coordination" on top of systems of record. [S] — [a16z Part 2](https://a16z.com/newsletter/big-ideas-2026-part-2/), [podcast](https://a16z.com/podcast/big-ideas-2026-the-enterprise-orchestration-layer/)
- YC Summer 2026 1.2 "Company Brain / AI OS for companies". [S] — [VC Cafe](https://www.vccafe.com/requests-for-startups-summer-2026-edition/)
- Menlo prediction: explainability/governance (audit logs for agent decisions) becomes mainstream in 2026. [P] — [Menlo 2025 report](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)
- Timing: big-company build-out, hard for solo entrants; governance/audit-log tooling is a smaller niche.

**T4. Voice agents (wedge -> full workflow)**
- a16z "Big Ideas 2026: Voice Agents and High-Stakes Trust"; Part 2 says many firms are in a "voice-as-a-wedge" phase and will expand into full workflows. [S] — [a16z podcast](https://a16z.com/podcast/big-ideas-2026-voice-agents-and-high-stakes-trust/)
- Olivia Moore (a16z) end-2025 view: voice agents moving from demos to scale; healthcare, banking, recruiting lead. Example: Toma AI (car dealerships), a16z-funded June 2025. [S] — [Specter](https://insights.tryspecter.com/ai-voice-agent-2025-a16z-landscape/), [Techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/toma-ai-secures-funding-from-a16z)
- YC Fall 2026 "AI for the Aging Population" includes voice interfaces. [P] — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)
- Entry: SMB vertical voice (clinics, dealers, home services) still open but crowded with funded players.

**T5. Agentic commerce and agent payment rails (ACP / UCP / AP2)**
- ACP (OpenAI+Stripe, Sept 2025); UCP (Google+Shopify, NRF Jan 2026; backers reportedly Walmart, Target, Etsy, PayPal, Visa, Stripe, Amex); AP2 (Google; reportedly donated to FIDO Alliance 28 Apr 2026). [S, vendor/agency blogs] — [ecorpit comparison](https://ecorpit.com/ucp-acp-ap2-agentic-commerce-standards-merchant-guide-2026/), [Weaverse](https://weaverse.io/blogs/agentic-commerce-acp-ucp-2026)
- Claim that only ~12 Shopify merchants had enabled ACP Instant Checkout by March 2026 and OpenAI narrowed ACP to discovery; UCP directory >8,000 verified stores by June 2026. [U: single-source blogs] — [UCP Checker](https://ucpchecker.com/blog/state-of-agentic-commerce-june-2026)
- Several sources say mid-market merchants will not implement protocols directly; platforms/payment providers do. [S] — same ecorpit link. Implication: opportunity is catalog/feed quality and tooling, not protocol implementation.
- YC Fall 2026 "Best Time to Build in Crypto" lists stablecoin rails and agentic commerce. [P] — [YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs)

**T6. AI-search visibility / GEO (generative engine optimization)**

[S: TechCrunch reporting Adobe, not Adobe's release] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/ai-traffic-to-us-retailers-rose-393-in-q1-and-its-boosting-their-revenue-too/)
- Caveat: base is small; no verified absolute share of retail traffic found.
- Gartner (2024) predicted traditional search volume -25% by 2026; Search Engine Land notes it was a press-release guess without measured backing. [S] — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/?p=437650). Reliable GEO market-size figure: not found [U].
- Entry: low barrier, high vendor crowding; agency/SMB service angle viable.

**T7. MCP / agent tooling ecosystem**

**T8. Vertical AI (healthcare, legal, etc.) and AI-for-SMB back office**

[P] — [Menlo](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)

- MIT NANDA: vendor-bought tools succeed ~67% vs internal builds ~1/3 as often; strongest ROI in back office though most budget goes to sales/marketing. [S] — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/)

**T9. Procurement/field-ops agents with network moats (a16z Speedrun, Troy Kirwin)**

**T10. Proactive/consumer AI beyond the prompt box, and cheap consumer AI products**

- Coding is the largest departmental category: ~$4.0B (55% of departmental spend), up from ~$550M; ~50% of developers use AI coding tools daily; Anthropic ~54% of coding LLM share. [P] — [Menlo](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)

### Inferences
- The strongest cross-source convergence (YC, Sequoia-via-secondary, a16z Speedrun, ARK context) is T1 services-as-software plus T8 SMB back office; these are also the clearest places for a small team.
- Agent-facing infrastructure (T2, T5, T7) is where the "picks and shovels" demand is stated, but protocols are dominated by large platforms; small-team value is in vertical connectors, catalog/data readiness, and monitoring.
- GEO has the only hard demand data (Adobe) but the base is small and the space is crowded with vendors.

## What data shows adoption or demand?

### Takeaway
Adoption is broad but shallow: most firms use AI, few have scaled agents. Spend and AI-referred commerce traffic are growing fast from small bases.

### Cited Findings
- McKinsey State of AI 2025: 88% use AI in at least one function (78% prior year); 7% fully scaled, 31% scaling; 23% scaling an agentic system somewhere, 39% experimenting; no more than 10% scaling agents in any single function; agent use mostly IT and knowledge management. [S: search summary of McKinsey page] — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/the-state-of-ai-2025)
- Menlo: only 16% of enterprise and 27% of startup deployments qualify as true agents (plan, act, observe, adapt); agent platforms ~$750M, ~10% of horizontal AI spend. [P] — [Menlo](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)
- Gartner (Aug 2025): 40% of enterprise apps will have task-specific agents by end of 2026, up from <5% in 2025; best case agentic AI ~30% of enterprise app software revenue (>$450B) by 2035. [P: press release in results] — [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-08-26-gartner-predicts-40-percent-of-enterprise-apps-will-feature-task-specific-ai-agents-by-2026-up-from-less-than-5-percent-in-2025.)
- Salesforce (not Adobe): AI agents influenced ~20% of global online retail sales in holiday 2025 (~$262B); sources conflict on wording (influenced vs orders). [U] — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/364733/ai-traffic-us-retailers-jumps-q1-agentic-shoppers-outspend-humans)
- Adobe data: see T6.

### Inferences
- Gartner's "40% of apps" is embedded-agent penetration, which is a weaker claim than autonomous agent deployment; McKinsey and Menlo show real autonomy is far lower.

- Primary McKinsey/Adobe/Salesforce pages not opened directly.

## What are skeptics saying?

### Takeaway
Credible skeptics focus on agent reliability, cost and "agent washing"; the 95%-fail and 40%-cancelled headlines are narrower than usually quoted.

### Cited Findings
- Gartner (June 2025): over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end of 2027 due to cost, unclear value, weak risk controls; analyst Anushree Verma: mostly hype-driven early experiments; only ~130 of thousands of "agentic" vendors are real (agent washing). [S, consistent across outlets] — [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/deeptech/artificial-intelligence/over-40-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-scrapped-by-2027-says-gartner)
- MIT NANDA "GenAI Divide" (Aug 2025): ~95% of pilots show no measurable P&L impact; based on 150 interviews, 350 employee survey, 300 deployments; cause framed as a "learning gap". Menlo's report explicitly disputes the bubble framing. [S] — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/), [letsdatascience on Menlo](https://letsdatascience.com/news/enterprises-increase-ai-spending-to-37bn-6d854973)
- Gary Marcus: agents brittle, hallucination unsolved. [S/opinion] — [Marcus](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/ai-agents-hype-versus-reality-redux)
- Compounding-error argument (99% per step across many steps); coding agents conceded as exception. [S/opinion] — [Artanis](https://artanis.substack.com/p/ai-is-still-a-bubble)
- Stuart Russell warned of a possible confidence collapse like the 1980s AI winter. [S] — [Benton](https://www.benton.org/headlines/revenge-ai-bubble)
- Protocol hype check: ACP reportedly narrowed after weak merchant uptake. [U] (see T5)

### Inferences
- Skeptic data supports betting on narrow, bounded-scope, auditable workflows (services with human review) rather than open-ended autonomy.

### Gaps
- No clearly dated Oct 2026 skeptic events found; the skeptic sources are 2025 to early 2026. HN discussions not searched.

## Which trends are early enough for a solo/small team?

### Takeaway
Best fits (judgment, not sourced forecast): vertical AI-native services for SMB back office (T1/T8), agent-facing tooling and niche MCP connectors (T2/T7), GEO/AI-readiness services for merchants (T6), and a16z-style "solo founder" agents (T8). Poor fits: horizontal orchestration, protocol-building, foundation-adjacent consumer apps.

### Cited Findings
- a16z Speedrun explicitly solicits solo-founder tooling and procurement agents; YC lists "Selling to Huge Companies" for 2-3 person teams. [S] — [VC Cafe](https://www.vccafe.com/requests-for-startups-summer-2026-edition/)
- Menlo: product-led growth drives 27% of AI app spend vs 7% in traditional software; startups hold ~63% of the application layer. [P] — [Menlo](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/)
- Adobe: roughly a quarter to a third of retail content is not AI-readable. [S] — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/ai-traffic-to-us-retailers-rose-393-in-q1-and-its-boosting-their-revenue-too/)

### Inferences
- Gaps in AI-readiness (Adobe) and bought-not-built preference (Menlo 76%, MIT 67% success) favour packaged vertical offerings from small vendors.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on solo-founder success rates or time-to-saturation per trend.

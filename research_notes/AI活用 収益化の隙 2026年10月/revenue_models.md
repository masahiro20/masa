# How solo founders and tiny teams make money with AI in 2026: cases, outcome distribution, pricing, channels

Research date: 2026-10-14. About 25 tool calls, mostly web search summaries plus direct fetches of TrustMRR, Stripe, and Growth Unhinged pages. Verification tiers used below:
- A = payment-processor-verified (TrustMRR Stripe API key, or journalist-inspected books)
- B = founder self-reported publicly (X, Indie Hackers), not independently checked
- C = blog or aggregator claim only

Limits of this pass: search tools returned summaries, not raw pages. Reddit, HN, Starter Story and Lenny's Newsletter yielded no usable primary posts. Several TrustMRR URLs I guessed (headshotpro, photoai, gojiberryai) returned 404. Team size is "not listed" on most TrustMRR pages, so "solo" cannot be confirmed for the larger cases.

## Q1. Which cases have primary-source revenue proof vs. only blog claims? (10-13 concrete cases)

### Takeaway
TrustMRR (Stripe-API-verified) gives Tier A proof for a handful of AI products, from $1.3K to $864K MRR. Most famous "solo AI" stories (Pieter Levels, Marc Lou, agency retainers, MCP servers, HeadshotPro) are Tier B or C. The one fully journalist-inspected mega-case (Medvi) is disputed and is not really a solo AI-product case.

### Cited Findings

**Tier A: TrustMRR, revenue verified via Stripe API key**

   - Pricing: Hobby $40/mo, Standard $150/mo, Pro $500/mo, Enterprise custom; annual is 20% off.

   - Founder has 23.4k X followers (distribution via build-in-public). Team not listed. — [TrustMRR Postiz](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz)
   - The leaderboard showed 22% MoM growth. — [TrustMRR leaderboard](https://trustmrr.com)

### Inferences
- The only dependable pattern across Tier A cases: per-seat or tiered SaaS subscriptions ($29-$500/mo), many small customers (7.7K and 12.4K subs), and a founder with a public audience (Postiz 23K X followers).
- Cases 7-13 should be labelled "founder-reported" or "unverified" in any report.
- Many large TrustMRR winners were founded before the 2025-26 vibe-coding wave (Chatbase 2023), so "AI makes it easy now" is not demonstrated by that data.

### Gaps
- No verified cases found for: marketplaces, data products, newsletters, productized services, template or Claude-skill sales. Only Tier C anecdotes exist for MCP/skills.
- Team size is unconfirmed for Chatbase, Postiz, GojiberryAI and others.
- Acquisition channel per case is mostly not stated on TrustMRR.
- No Indie Hackers verified-revenue, Starter Story, Lenny's, Reddit or HN primary posts were retrieved. A Starter Story search returned only a generic idea page, with an "average $56.7K/mo" figure for AI storytelling apps and no named founder.
- Time-to-first-revenue is available only in aggregate (below).

## Q2. What is the realistic distribution of outcomes? (median revenue, failure, churn; debunking inflated claims)

### Takeaway
Revenue is extremely concentrated: on TrustMRR the median projects with revenue earn about $445 MRR, while 103 outliers average $102K. AI-native products also churn much more than traditional SaaS unless priced above about $250/mo. The "$20K/month n8n agency" claims are unsourced marketing.

### Cited Findings

**Distribution (TrustMRR, 986 solo startups, article Mar 9 2026)**
- Outliers above about $16.4K MRR: 103 startups; median $37K MRR, average $102K. — [onemilliongoal](https://www.onemilliongoal.com/p/what-986-solo-startups-actually-look)
- Core group (positive revenue, below threshold): 528 startups, median $445 MRR; about 27% under $100/mo; over half under $500; only 13% above $5K. — same source
- Subtraction suggests about 355 of 986 (36%) fall outside both groups, possibly zero revenue; the article does not confirm this. — same source
- Marc Lou's live-platform figures (Nov 2025 post): 81% made $1+; 44% made $1,000+; 4% made $1M+; average time to first $1 is 5 months; median total revenue per startup $1,618. — same source
- Margins: median 81% among 464 reporting. 38% of core projects are listed for sale, median asking price $30,250. — same source
- Caveat: TrustMRR is self-selected (founders opt in), so it skews toward builders who want visibility. "MRR" there is last-30-day revenue, not strictly recurring. — [TrustMRR via search](https://trustmrr.com/startup/indiemerger?metric=mrr)

**Stripe Atlas (Stripe's own blog, May 28 2026)**
- Solo founders were 63% of Atlas C-corps formed in Q2 2026, an all-time high.
- Among solo startups, median six-month revenue in 2025 fell 23% YoY while the top decile rose 19%; the top-decile-to-median ratio went from about 34x to 61x.
- Solopreneurs earning over $100K/yr rose by a third since 2022. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/top-solo-founder-traits)
- Top-decile traits: AI-native products (2x as likely; by year two AI-native solos made almost twice the revenue of other solos), global sales from launch (51% of revenue international vs. 2% median), B2B focus (median B2B revenue over 4x median B2C by month 24), and recurring billing with early retention (about 30% of month-1 customers returned vs. 8% middle decile). — same source
- By month 24, top-decile multi-founder startups out-earned top-decile solos by 53%, though the gap shrinks to 5% at the 99th percentile of bootstrapped startups. — same source
- Conflicting: a macmdviewer roundup says the median Atlas startup earned 39% more in 2025; it covers all Atlas startups, not just solos. — [macmdviewer](https://macmdviewer.com/blog/solo-founder-ai-stack-statistics-2026)

**Churn (ChartMogul data via Growth Unhinged, Dec 10 2025; about 3,500 companies, about 200 AI-native, retention analysis limited to those with at least $250K ARR)**
- AI-native median GRR 40%, NRR 48%, versus B2B SaaS NRR 82% and B2C SaaS NRR 49%. AI-native GRR improved from 27% (Jan) to 40% (Sep 2025).
- By price: above $250/mo GRR 70% / NRR 85%; $50-$249 GRR 45% / NRR 61%; under $50 GRR 23% / NRR 32%.
- Annual plans show NRR 10-20 points higher than monthly. Some buckets have only about 50 companies, so the author calls it directional. — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-ai-churn-wave)
- Survivorship bias: the sample includes only companies above $250K ARR, so tiny solo products likely look worse.

**Failure rates (secondary only)**
- CB Insights-based claim: over 14,000 AI startups launched in 2024, about 3,800 shut down by end of 2025 (27%). A separate "3% wrapper survival over two years, McKinsey" claim is unverified and measures something different. — [aiagentrank / betonai-style roundups via search](https://betonai.net/ai-tools-that-were-hyped-in-2025-but-dead-by-2026-the-saas-casualties/)
- Wrapper failure mechanisms cited: platform copying (ChatGPT adding the feature), zero switching costs, model price drops, and enterprise pilots that never converted. — [alexcloudstar](https://www.alexcloudstar.com/blog/ai-wrappers-dead-what-to-build-instead-2026/)

**Debunking agency claims**
- Claims such as "$40K/month with five clients", "$25K MRR in four months", and "$20K+/month n8n agency" cite no verifiable source. — [theaiimplementationmethod](https://theaiimplementationmethod.com/2026/05/19/n8n-automation-agency-business/); [AFFStudio guide](https://affstudio.org/2026/08/20/how-to-start-an-n8n-automation-agency-in-2026-services-pricing-and-first-clients/)
- The more modest AFFStudio guide itself says its best-documented case is a single unverified anecdote of about $8,200/mo, and puts first-year solo realism at roughly $2-5K/mo, $5-15K/mo for niche specialists.
- Commodity floor: Fiverr automation gigs start at $20-$100; Gumroad sells $15-$70 bundles of "thousands" of n8n workflows. — same AFFStudio source
- The "fifth retainer client" bottleneck is opinion, not data. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@automation.labs/ai-automation-agencies-charge-5k-a-client-few-get-five-f0e9ad0b5dde)

### Inferences
- Realistic expectation for a solo AI SaaS: most make under $500 MRR; a low-single-digit percent exceed $16K MRR. The "$445 median" applies only to those with revenue, and is a selected sample.
- The 40% GRR figure applies to companies already above $250K ARR, so true small-product retention is probably worse, especially under $50/mo.
- Agency claims above about $10K/mo should be treated as unverified. A plausible realistic band is $2-8K/mo in year one, per the one guide that hedges.

### Gaps
- No agency-specific distribution, failure, or churn data from any primary source.
- No median MRR dataset specific to AI-native indie products.
- No Reddit or HN threads were retrieved, so no firsthand post-mortems.
- The original CB Insights and McKinsey reports were not checked.

## Q3. Which pricing models work (usage-based, outcome-based, flat retainer)?

### Takeaway
Evidence favors higher price points and recurring or annual billing; flat monthly retainers dominate agencies. Outcome-based pricing evidence was not found; usage-based evidence is limited to anecdotes.

### Cited Findings
- Price level is the strongest retention lever: above $250/mo GRR 70%; under $50/mo GRR 23%. Annual plans add 10-20 NRR points. — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-ai-churn-wave)
- Top-decile Atlas solos were 26 points (B2B) and 20 points (B2C) more likely to use recurring billing. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/top-solo-founder-traits)
- Chatbase's tiers ($40 / $150 / $500 / custom, 12.4K subs, $864K MRR) show the successful ladder: free tier, then three paid tiers, annual discount 20%. — [TrustMRR Chatbase](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatbase)
- Postiz uses plans from $29/mo with a 7-day trial. — [TrustMRR Postiz](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz)
- Agency pricing patterns: n8n project fees $2K-$8K plus retainers $500-$2K/mo (guide). An unverified case uses $8K setup plus $3K/mo. — [AFFStudio](https://affstudio.org/2026/08/20/how-to-start-an-n8n-automation-agency-in-2026-services-pricing-and-first-clients/); [beginnersinai.org](https://beginnersinai.org/ai-agency-success-story/)
- Productized packages are credited over hourly billing, which "commoditizes expertise" (opinion). — same beginnersinai source
- MCP/skills: usage-gated freemium (free local, paid hosted at about $19/mo) is the commonly proposed pattern; one vendor mentions an 80/20 marketplace split. — [openclawlaunch](https://openclawlaunch.com/blog/mcp-skills-monetization-guide)

### Inferences
- The Tier A data supports subscription tiers that reach $40-$500 rather than sub-$20 plans; the churn data explains why.
- Outcome-based pricing is a gap in my evidence and should not be recommended on this research alone.

### Gaps
- No verified usage-based or outcome-based pricing case with revenue.
- Annual-vs-monthly mix of the TrustMRR winners not known.

## Q4. Which distribution channels convert?

### Takeaway
The only channel with direct support in the verified data is founder-led audience building on X; agency channel evidence is a single unverified claim that referrals worked and cold LinkedIn did not. SEO/GEO, Reddit and cold-email conversion evidence was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Postiz founder has a 23.4K-follower X account; StartClaw's founder has 1.6K (and $1.3K MRR). Correlation only. — [TrustMRR Postiz](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz); [TrustMRR StartClaw](https://trustmrr.com/startup/startclaw)
- StartClaw's domain rating of 15/100 shows it has little SEO authority yet. — [TrustMRR StartClaw](https://trustmrr.com/startup/startclaw)
- Top-decile solo founders sold into about 10 countries in month 1, and international sales were 51% of revenue (median 2%). This suggests internet-native, language-light distribution rather than local sales. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/blog/top-solo-founder-traits)
- One agency case: 5 of 7 clients from referrals by client #1; cold LinkedIn gave 0% response (single unverified anecdote). — [beginnersinai.org](https://beginnersinai.org/ai-agency-success-story/)
- MCP registry: official registry is "good for discovery, not revenue" (vendor blog opinion). — [openclawlaunch](https://openclawlaunch.com/blog/mcp-skills-monetization-guide)
- TrustMRR itself is a distribution channel: Marc Lou's platform ranks startups, and the open feed posts founder claims. — [TrustMRR via search](https://trustmrr.com/open)

### Inferences
- Build-in-public on X plus a public revenue leaderboard seems to be the strongest observed loop, but I have no conversion data.
- For agencies, referral and warm networks outperform cold outreach in the little evidence available.

### Gaps
- No conversion-rate data for SEO/GEO, Reddit, cold email, or marketplaces.
- No channel attribution in any Tier A case.

## Aggregated patterns by business model (from the above, low-confidence where marked)

- Micro-SaaS / SaaS with AI features: best-evidenced model (Chatbase, Postiz, StartClaw). Wide spread: $1.3K to $864K MRR. Works with B2B, mid-priced tiers, recurring billing, and public distribution. High churn if under $50/mo.
- API wrappers: documented as failing when the platform adds the feature natively. Chatbase succeeded as a vertical wrapper (support chatbots), so a defensible niche, data, or distribution matters. Evidence is blog-level.
- AI / automation agencies: plausible $2-15K/mo; high-end claims unverified; the retention advantage is retainers; commodity pressure from Fiverr and Gumroad templates.
- Productized services, newsletters, data products, marketplaces: no verified AI-specific case found in this pass.
- MCP servers / skills / templates: only anecdotes ($2K-$10K/mo claims) and a claim that under 5% of 12,000+ servers monetize (unsourced). Tier C.
- Regulated or high-risk arbitrage (Medvi): large revenue but disputed and legally exposed; not a model to recommend.

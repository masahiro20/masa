# Underserved AI-business gaps for a solo/tiny-team operator in Japan (as of Oct 2026)

Evidence-quality labels: [P] primary/government/official; [V] vendor or vendor-run media (biased); [N] news; [S] secondary explainer/law-firm slides; [A] anecdotal/listing. Research was limited (about 17 tool calls; search returned summaries, not full pages, except where noted). Demand evidence for most gaps is indirect. Nothing below is a validated demand signal; all candidates need a 1-2 week smoke test.

## Q1. What Japanese SMB / market context supports AI-service demand, and what do SMBs pay (price anchors in JPY)?

### Takeaway
SMB generative-AI adoption is still low-to-middling and fragmented by survey, so "implementation help" demand exists, but pricing anchors that I could verify are mostly vendor-published: LLMO/GEO from about JPY 10k-15k/mo (tool) to JPY 50k-3M/mo (services); subsidy-application agency fees 0-300k upfront plus 8-20% success fee; invoice/receipt AI-OCR from JPY 2,000-5,000/mo.

### Cited Findings
- Daido Life survey: 60% of SMEs have never used generative AI; 13% use daily. Tokyo Chamber of Commerce survey: 83.9% of (mostly Tokyo 23-ward, 1,272) firms use generative AI. Different samples/definitions, not comparable. [N] — [OANDA/news summary](https://www.oanda.jp/lab-education/market_news/kn_2026031101001102/)
- A survey by 情報通信総合研究所 reportedly shows adoption falling with firm size (about 2 in 10 at 100-299 employees). Secondhand via search summary. [S] — [ascii.jp](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/390/4390591/)
- The 2026 中小企業白書 (METI/SME Agency), Part 2 Ch.2 Sec.2, frames "optimizing labor input" (labor-saving investment, AI use, digitalization) as the response to structural labor shortage. I could not retrieve the white paper's own AI adoption figure. [P, via summary] — [newswitch](https://newswitch.jp/p/49556)
- LLMO/GEO pricing (Jinrai blog; vendor with own tool, so biased): free single checks; JPY 10-20k/mo multi-AI monitoring tools; JPY 20k-100k/mo monitoring+execution support; JPY 200k+/mo consulting. Illustrative service tiers: Small JPY 50-100k/mo (+100k setup, 3 mo), Standard 150-300k/mo (+200k setup, 6 mo), Full 500k-1M/mo, Enterprise 1-3M/mo. Article states no industry-standard price list exists. Their own tool GEO Hack Suite: JPY 14,800/mo (BYOK from 9,800); paid diagnosis report JPY 55,000; GEO rewrite of existing article from JPY 10,000; new article from JPY 44,000; QA page JPY 110,000. [V] — [Jinrai](https://jinrai.co.jp/blog/2026/04/18/llmo-ryoukin-sohba/); also [Jinrai tools guide](https://jinrai.co.jp/blog/2026/07/22/llmo-tools-guide/)
- A comparison of 13 LLMO services exists on IT Trend (competition is already present). [S] — [IT Trend](https://it-trend.jp/llm-optimization-services/24009/price)
- Subsidy application agency fees: typical upfront 0-300k JPY plus success fee ~10-20% of awarded amount; one 行政書士 corporation advertises 0 upfront / 8-15% success fee covering ものづくり and 新事業進出 subsidies. 新事業進出補助金 cited up to JPY 70M (90M with large wage hikes), unverified against 公募要領. [V] — [office-tree](https://office-tree.jp/blog/subsidy/subsidy-application-success-fee-only/), [atsoho](https://atsoho.com/blog/subsidy-daikou-hiyou-2026), [shigyo.co.jp](https://www.shigyo.co.jp/search_post/business-subsidy/%e4%b8%ad%e5%b0%8f%e4%bc%81%e6%a5%ad%e6%96%b0%e4%ba%8b%e6%a5%ad%e9%80%b2%e5%87%ba%e8%a3%9c%e5%8a%a9%e9%87%91/)
- Subsidy-writing outsourcing quoted at JPY 50k-300k per application; AI alone rarely reaches adoption, human+AI recommended. [V] — [atsoho](https://atsoho.com/blog/subsidy-application-creation-service-ai)
- AI subsidy drafting tools already exist: 「補助金AI」(Mycat), 「補助金Flash」 (generates 新事業進出補助金 plans; free trial). [V] — [atsoho comparison](https://atsoho.com/blog/subsidy-application-support-business-plan-ai-compare)
- Invoice/e-bookkeeping SaaS anchor: 経費BANK receipt AI-OCR from JPY 2,000/mo, invoice AI-OCR from JPY 5,000/mo (ex-tax), 1,500+ customers. [V/PR] — [ECnomikata PR](https://ecnomikata.com/pr/42431)
- Shopify has built-in free AI product descriptions (Shopify Magic) with Japanese page showing description generation supported; third-party apps often English-only or require own Gemini API key; ChatGPT-description app USD 10/mo. Amazon has seller generative AI tools. Rakuten announced description auto-creation (2019 plan; current status unverified). [V/official app pages] — [AIsmiley](https://aismiley.co.jp/ai_news/amazon-generative-ai-tool-seller/), [Shopify Magic](https://apps.shopify.com/built-in-features/magic-product-descriptions?locale=ja), [Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/chatgpt?locale=ja)

### Inferences
- Platform-native AI (Shopify Magic, Amazon) commoditizes plain product-description generation; EC gap, if any, is cross-platform ops (Rakuten RMS/Yahoo/Amazon listing sync, compliance wording checks such as 薬機法/景表法) rather than text generation. Unverified.
- Price ladder suggests micro-SaaS at JPY 3k-15k/mo is the credible self-serve band; JPY 50k+/mo requires human-assisted services.

### Gaps
- No verified Japan SMB willingness-to-pay data for clinic/restaurant AI receptionists (search found no JPY pricing), freee/Money Forward/Bill One current prices, or EC-tool pricing. 白書 AI adoption number not retrieved.

## Q2. Candidate gaps (8-12) with demand evidence, competition, why incumbents miss it, automatability

### Takeaway
Best-supported by evidence: (1) GEO/LLMO for local/small Japanese businesses (a real paid market with a price ladder but few self-serve low-end offerings found), (2) Japan-data MCP servers/skills (supply exists but is thin, duplicative and unmonetized), (3) inbound-tourism AI data/content for overseas buyers (growing capital flowing in), (4) compliance-safe AI for 士業 workflows (new MOJ guidance lowers a barrier). Others are plausible but evidence is thin.

### Cited Findings (evidence per candidate; evidence quality labelled)
1. GEO/LLMO for small Japanese businesses (clinics, restaurants, local shops, SMB EC). Demand: multiple price-ladder articles; 13-service comparison page; vendors selling JPY 14.8k/mo tools. Competition: moderate and rising at JPY 100k+/mo agency tier; low-priced (<JPY 10k/mo) vertical packages not seen in my results. Automatable: monitoring citations across ChatGPT/Gemini/AI Overviews, structured-data (JSON-LD) generation, monthly reports are automatable. [V] — [Jinrai](https://jinrai.co.jp/blog/2026/04/18/llmo-ryoukin-sohba/), [IT Trend](https://it-trend.jp/llm-optimization-services/24009/price). Caveat: the article states results take 3-6+ months; sources are vendor-biased and efficacy of GEO is unproven.
2. Japan-specific MCP servers / agent skills (e-Gov laws, 法人番号, gBizINFO, tax/invoice-number lookups, Tabelog). Evidence: several e-Gov law MCP servers already on Glama/LobeHub (no API key needed); 法人番号 Web-API requires free app ID; gBizINFO needs token; one implementation admits CSV column mapping unverified against real API; a Tabelog restaurant MCP (gurume) exists. Competition: many hobby repos, near-duplicates, no clear paid product. Terms of service for redistribution/commercial use not verified. [A/S] — [Glama hourei](https://glama.ai/mcp/servers/@groundcobra009/hourei-mcp-server/blob/59fded68a2e73edbc36cfaceac57f1d0b59f97f9/README.md), [LobeHub e-Gov](https://lobehub.com/mcp/go-555-e-gov-mcp), [gurume](https://pypi.org/project/gurume/). Free commodity layer; money is more plausibly in hosted, maintained, quality-checked bundles (e.g., updated 公募要領/補助金 data, 税制 rule packs) than in the thin API wrappers.
3. Inbound tourism data/content/booking for overseas buyers. Evidence: byFood (inbound food experiences) raised JPY 1.34B Series B (Sept 2026), planning AI personalization/reservations/multilingual; a 2024 inbound figure of 36.87M cited by an AI reservation vendor (verify vs JNTO); staff shortage of English speakers drives AI language tools. Competition: funded players on booking side; content/data niches (regional restaurant/izakaya multilingual menus, B2B data feeds for overseas agents) not assessed. [N/V] — [The Bridge](https://thebridge.jp/en/2026/09/food-experience-platform-byfood-for-inbound-tourists-raises-%c2%a51-34-billion-in-series-b), [TTG Asia (2023)](https://www.ttgasia.com/2023/10/24/japan-leverages-on-ai-to-ease-communication-hurdles/).
4. AI legal-support/士業-adjacent tools for corporate legal (contract review, research, internal investigation). Evidence: MOJ published (2026-08-21) new guideline "ビジネス分野におけるAI等法務業務支援サービス提供と弁護士法第72条の関係について" supplementing the 2023 guideline; 7 service types listed as not conflicting (research, drafting/review of documents, compliance, whistleblower investigations, internal investigations, meeting support, etc.), 2 types potentially conflicting (court-filing-document-specialized design; continuing to provide while knowing/accepting improper use). Governance measures (disclaimers, ToS, referral-to-lawyer notices) function as evidence of no intent. [S: law-firm slides summarizing P] — [Miyake slides](https://www.miyake.gr.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AI法務支援サービスと弁護士法72条_法務省GL解説1.pdf), [MOJ](https://www.moj.go.jp/housei/shihouseido/housei10_00134.html). Competition: Money Forward, LegalOn etc. are strong in contracts (not researched in depth here).
5. Subsidy (補助金) application support (AI-assisted, human/士業-partnered). Evidence above (JPY 50-300k per application; 8-20% success fees; AI draft tools exist). Competition: crowded at the agency level; AI tools exist (補助金AI, 補助金Flash). Regulatory: 行政書士法 restricts non-licensed preparation of documents for government for pay; one article discusses 業際問題 and fee appropriateness of outsourcing subsidy support to non-士業. Best fit is selling software/templates/matching to 行政書士/認定支援機関 rather than doing applications yourself. [V] — [atsoho on 業際](https://atsoho.com/blog/subsidy-application-support-outsourcing)
6. AI phone/reception for clinics and restaurants. Evidence: Japanese vendors exist (e.g., 「もしもしAI」 by Exatech, NTT AI telephone reservation, 「AIスタッフさゆり」), comparison articles for clinics; Retell AI has Japanese landing pages; Leadsia (Feb 2026, vendor) argues voice AI in Japanese is a high barrier. Pricing not found. Competition: NTT/LY Corp-class and startups; a solo builder likely loses on telephony integration unless niche (specific POS/reservation systems). [V] — [Sakigake](https://www.sakigake.jp/news/article/20260616PT0003/), [NTT Com](https://www.ntt.com/business/services/aitelephone/lp/column-12.html), [Leadsia PDF](https://gjymqliqzutkluawtpjo.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-pdfs/japanese-ai-wall.pdf)
7. Invoice/電帳法 compliance automation. Evidence: 電子取引データ保存 mandatory since Jan 2024; 2026 explainers say focus shifted to 経理DX; price anchors JPY 2-5k/mo AI-OCR. Competition: very high (freee, Money Forward, Bill One, 経費BANK etc.). A claim of "インボイス制度2026年10月改正" came from an unverified blog and is not confirmed. [V/S] — [ECnomikata](https://ecnomikata.com/pr/42431), [aurant](https://aurant-technologies.com/?p=2974). Likely a poor entry point except thin vertical add-ons.
8. Overseas-product localization arbitrage (Japan-language/Japan-compliant clones). Evidence is thin: Leadsia (vendor, Feb 2026) cites Zoom, Asana, HubSpot as foreign SaaS that struggled in Japan; CSA Research says Japanese localization often falls short, recommending transcreation; Pendo localized for Japan. I found no list of specific overseas AI products lacking a Japanese equivalent. [V/S] — [Leadsia PDF](https://gjymqliqzutkluawtpjo.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-pdfs/japanese-ai-wall.pdf), [CSA Research](https://csa-research.com/Blogs-Events/Blog/Six-Steps-to-Ensure-Japanese-Linguistic-Quality), [Pendo](https://jp.pendo.io/pendo-blog/pendo-localizes-for-japan)
9. Japan market research / translation / data for English-speaking buyers. Evidence: Upwork listings at about USD 15-50/hr for EN-JA localization and market research; a consultant (self-promotional) calls Japan a hardest market to enter due to Japanese-only documentation and long sales cycles. No sizing of demand. [A] — [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/marketing-japan-market-research-on-competitors-buyers-influencers-and-messaging-2063639489045274265), [CSA](https://csa-research.com/Blogs-Events/Blog/Six-Steps-to-Ensure-Japanese-Linguistic-Quality)
10. AI-dubbing/localization market (Japan anime/content to overseas). Global AI dubbing and localization market forecast USD 600.8M (2025) to USD 8.16B (2035), CAGR 29.8% (market-research firm forecast, not Japan-specific, low reliability). [V] — [Astute Analytica via DreamNews](https://www.dreamnews.jp/press/0000361174)
11. EC cross-platform ops (Rakuten/Amazon JP/Shopify JP) AI: see Q1; native tools commoditize copy generation; gap unverified.
12. Data-privacy-compliant AI workflows (APPI-aware templates, clinics/medical). See Q3. Evidence of demand: law was just amended; no direct demand data.

### Inferences
- Most defensible solo plays: GEO audits/monitoring productized at JPY 5k-50k/mo for local businesses; Japan-data/skill bundles sold to developers or as hosted API; B2B content/data for inbound travel agencies; selling tooling to 士業 rather than doing 士業 work.
- Highly crowded (avoid head-on): invoice/電帳法 SaaS, generic subsidy-writing, generic AI reception.

### Gaps
- No Japanese Product Hunt-equivalent (e.g., Product Hunt Japan, PR Times launch lists) was analyzed; no Nikkei/ITmedia sweep; no Zenn/Qiita/note complaint-volume data; no search-volume data (Ahrefs MCP unauthorized). No verified list of overseas AI products without Japanese equivalents. No restaurant/clinic/real-estate-specific pricing found.

## Q3. Regulatory constraints

### Takeaway
弁護士法72条 now has fresher MOJ guidance (Aug 2026) with safe-harbor-like categories; APPI was amended July 2026 (looser for AI development, with surcharges for large-scale misuse; enforcement within 2 years, by July 2028); ステマ規制 (景表法) applies to undisclosed paid reviews/ads, including possibly AI-generated affiliate content.

### Cited Findings
- 弁護士法72条 elements: non-lawyer, for compensation, in "一般の法律事件" (事件性: disputes nearly unavoidable per Supreme Court 2010-07-20), performing 鑑定/代理/仲裁/和解 etc., as a business. The 2026 guideline focuses on "value neutrality" judged by (1) design/function, (2) usage, (3) governance; user input can be attributed to the provider if the design automatically outputs legal work; court-filing-specialized design is out; knowing/accepting misuse is out. [S] — [Miyake slides](https://www.miyake.gr.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AI法務支援サービスと弁護士法72条_法務省GL解説1.pdf) (text extracted by pdftotext); also [MOJ](https://www.moj.go.jp/housei/shihouseido/housei10_00134.html) and [Money Forward explainer](https://biz.moneyforward.com/contract/basic/23024/)
- APPI amendment passed 2026-07-10: sensitive personal information (e.g., medical history) can be collected without consent for statistics/AI-development uses not tied to individuals, notably info publicly posted on SNS; surcharge system for large-scale (over 1,000 individuals) improper acquisition/use; enforcement within 2 years (by July 2028). Reports differ on third-party-provision scope; opposed by some medical bodies and parties; check statute text/PPC. [N] — [Kumanichi](https://kumanichi.com/articles/2012200), [Ben54](https://www.ben54.jp/news/3707), [Miyake APPI/GenAI](https://www.miyake.gr.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/解説資料：個人情報保護法改正と生成AI.pdf)
- ステマ規制 effective Oct 2023 as 景表法 notice; one explainer says AI-generated comparison/affiliate reviews can be covered if there is a compensation relationship (not an official CAA statement). Reports of a CAA 措置命令 in June 2026 unconfirmed. [S] — [aipicks glossary](https://aipicks.jp/glossary/stealth-marketing-regulation), [Money Forward](https://biz.moneyforward.com/contract/basic/8855/)
- Article-level text for 税理士法 (tax advice by AI), 行政書士法 on subsidy documents, 特商法 (subscription final-confirmation screen) and 資金決済法 was not found in this research.

### Inferences
- A product should be positioned as tool/information supply to licensed professionals or to the business's own staff, with disclaimers/ToS/referral-to-professional flows, which the MOJ guidance treats as evidence against intent. This is the researcher's reading, not legal advice.
- Selling subsidy application drafting to end customers for fees sits closest to 行政書士法 risk; selling software to 行政書士 does not.

### Gaps
- 税理士法52条/行政書士法/特商法/資金決済法 positions for AI services were not verified. Counsel review needed before launch.
